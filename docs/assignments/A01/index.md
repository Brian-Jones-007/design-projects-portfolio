## Decide
My homepage acts as the main landing pad for my portfolio, so I will design its layout around what a grader or prospective employer needs to see the second they arrive. Instead of cluttering the front page with a long bio, I will structure it to give immediate access to my course assignments, portfolio layout, and overall documentation standards. This allows a reviewer to quickly grasp how my work is organized, verify the criteria, and jump directly to any specific assignment without digging through unnecessary background information.

**Element Modified:** Updated the primary color scheme in `mkdocs.yml` from default `green` to `slate` with high-contrast `teal` accents  
**Requirement Satisfied:** Improves visual accessibility and legibility during technical reviews across varying display environments  
**Functional Reason:** The default bright blue color scheme produces high luminance, creating a potential glare when reviewing CAD renders and detailed technical diagrams. Switching to a slate background with high-contrast accents maintains clear readability and reduces reader eye strain during extended reviews.  

I am committed to producing clear, well-reasoned engineering documentation with stated equations and assumptions so that any classmate or engineer can understand and reproduce my work without needing to ask follow-up questions.
